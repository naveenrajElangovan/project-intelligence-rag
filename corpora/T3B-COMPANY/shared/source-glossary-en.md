# Authority glossary

- `INTERNAL_CATALOG`: exhaustive authority for active SKUs.
- `INTERNAL_SOP`: authority for departmental processes.
- `SEC_FILING`: public authority for structure and investor facts as of its reporting date.
- `OFFICIAL_WEB`: enrichment and terminology only; never SKU-population authority.
- `EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`: incomplete template content that must not become an assertion.
