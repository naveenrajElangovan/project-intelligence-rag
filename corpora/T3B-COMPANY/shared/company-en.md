# Tiendas 3B — shared public facts

## History and model {#history-model}

Tiendas 3B describes its format as hard discount. Its official site publishes history, business-model, and customer-experience pages. These materials provide terminology and context; they do not complete the SKU catalog.

Sources: https://www.tiendas3b.com/algo-de-nuestra-historia-3-b/ ; https://www.tiendas3b.com/modelo-hard-discount-3-b/ ; https://www.tiendas3b.com/mi-primera-vez/

## Public assortment and authority {#public-assortment}

The official category page advertises more than 800 products. The 2025 Form 20-F describes approximately 850–900 SKUs per store and short-rotation spot products. These are approximate figures and never replace the approved internal export.

Sources: https://tiendas3b.com/categorias/ ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1978954/000119312526140232/tbbb-20251231.htm

## Publicly documented organization {#organization}

The Form 20-F documents a store hierarchy including associate, assistant/store manager, district manager, zone manager, and regional director, plus distribution-center, purchasing, human-resources/Universidad 3B, real-estate, logistics, IT, and accounting functions. Internal procedures require internal SOP evidence.

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1978954/000119312526140232/tbbb-20251231.htm

## Financial freshness {#financial-freshness}

Current operating figures must be checked against the latest official release; for this cut, the freshness reference is the 2Q26 release. Figures are not duplicated here so a narrative document cannot silently become stale.
